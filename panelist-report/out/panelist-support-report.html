Date caveat carried through the whole report
No case-creation timestamp is present, so every time-based figure uses Modified On. That records the last time a case was touched, not when it arrived — a case opened in March and reopened in July counts as July. Trend direction and any "within N days" sequencing should be read with that distortion in mind.

Leading indicators worth instrumenting

  • Topic tags per case — currently 1.52. A rise means single contacts are absorbing more unresolved issues; it moves before handle time and before CSAT.
  • Share of volume from repeat members — 96.1%. Rising repeat share is the earliest sign that first-contact resolution is failing.
  • Reactivation and activity-inquiry share of primary category — the closest available proxy for panelists drifting toward involuntary purge.
  • Outbound / callback share — a rise indicates inbound channels are not closing issues on first contact.

If nothing changes

The fitted trend carries volume to roughly 1,442 cases in 2027Q2 (range 590–2,294) with the current mix intact — that is the do-nothing baseline against which any intervention should be measured.

5Strategic recommendations & action plan

Priority 1Quick win (0–30 days)Owner: Support Ops / CRM administration

Add 5 missing field(s) to the case export

The export currently supports volume and mix analysis but blocks several causal tests outright. Adding these fields costs a report definition change, not a system change: Created On / case-open timestamp — Every trend, seasonality and 'within N days' figure in this report is currently anchored to Modified On, which records last touch, not arrival.; Office / site — No site-level breakdown of volume or mix is possible.; Agent / case owner — No handling-side variance analysis is possible.; member type / household role (primary vs secondary) — Secondary-member lockout and household attrition are NOT computable without a member-type or household-link field.; enrollment or join date (for 30/60/90-day tenure) — Early-life churn is NOT computable without an enrollment date; tenure cannot be derived from case dates alone..

Ties to the NOT COMPUTABLE panels in sections 2 and 3.
Priority 1Quick win (0–30 days)Owner: Panel Operations

Attack the Attrition & Withdrawal driver first

Attrition & Withdrawal is the largest single primary category at 26.6% of cases (3579 of 13473). Any deflection built here has the widest reach; the associated exposure is direct panel shrinkage and replacement recruitment cost.

Ties to Finding 1 and section 1's category breakdown.
Priority 2This quarterOwner: Support Ops (taxonomy owner)

Split the multi-topic case into countable units

At 1.52 tags per case and 26.6% of cases multi-tagged, a single case can conceal an equipment fault, a reward dispute and a password reset at once. Either capture a required primary reason with explicit sub-reasons, or emit one case line per topic, so demand sizing and AHT attribution stop disagreeing.

Ties to Finding 2 and the tag-load table in section 1.
Priority 2This quarterOwner: WFM / Support Ops

Size deflection against Inbound Phone before adding headcount

52.1% of contacts arrive on Inbound Phone. Channel concentration at this level means capacity planning, IVR routing and self-service ROI all hinge on that single origin.

Ties to Finding 3 and section 1's origin split.
